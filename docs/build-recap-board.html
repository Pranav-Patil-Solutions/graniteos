The product — every module

🪨 Stock for stone

Block → numbered slabs, CBM, sq-ft, finish/grade/thickness, bundles, in-stock/reserved/sold, cost & profit per slab.

🧾 GST billing

Rule-46 tax invoice, CGST/SGST/IGST by place-of-supply, HSN/UQC, round-off, amount-in-words, FY numbering, GSTIN validation.

💰 Udhaar / money

Receivables ledger, per-invoice pending, payments (cash/UPI/bank/cheque), auto paid·partial·unpaid, UPI pay-links.

👥 Parties + history

Customers & suppliers, credit limits, GSTIN; full per-party ledger timeline (quote→order→invoice→payment).

📋 Quotes & orders

Builder with industry UOM, HSN, GST; editable quotes; order pipeline confirmed→dispatched→delivered.

🏭 Fabrication + Factory Floor

Production jobs through queued→cutting→polishing→edging→QC→ready→dispatched, by machine, with a live floor view. NEW

📐 Measurement Sheet

Slab-by-slab measured sizes feeding quotes, jobs and invoices. NEW

📦 Products + UOM

Reusable product master (rate, unit, HSN, GST); 10 stone-trade units.

📊 Owner analytics

Revenue, collected, receivables, stock value, 6-month trend, pending invoices, quote funnel, top customers.

🔗 Public catalogue

Shareable live-stock link, search/sort/filter, per-slab QR "passport", AR/3D try-on.

📣 Stock Alert

WhatsApp new-stock broadcast — one-tap (free) + hands-off Business-API auto-send; per-customer opt-in.

🎯 Marketing Helper

Auto-segments customers (hot leads, top, dormant, new, slow payers) → personalized one-tap WhatsApp.

🎙️ Voice control + Hindi

Speak to navigate & search; Latin→Hindi transliteration finds Hindi names; voice notes & daybook dictation. NEW

🤖 AI Studio

One home for AI tools — Slab Identifier, Room Visualizer, Copywriter — behind a billing gate. NEW

✅ Daybook to-do

Spoken/typed tasks with a confirm step, confetti on done, "carried N days" for unfinished, Morning Briefing. NEW

💳 Batch Payment

Pay a lump sum across a customer's unpaid invoices — oldest fills first, leftover shown. NEW

🕓 Activity Logs

Timestamped feed across every record — who did what, when. NEW

📸 Block photos

Real camera photos on stone blocks, replacing the initials chip in detail & list. NEW

🌗 Light/Dark + Mobile

Theme toggle (briefing card stays legible) and a phone layout with bottom tabs — choice survives reload. NEW

🔑 Product-key + seats

Per-seat product-key activation at company setup, with a go-live runbook. NEW

🛡️ Access control

3-state per-area permissions (No / View / Edit) with a server-side write-guard. NEW

⚙️ Team · import · onboarding

Roles & invites, Excel import wizard with per-row validation, guided onboarding, PWA + installer. NEW
