Readiness Scorecard

GST Invoicing Strong

Rule-46 compliant: per-line HSN, CGST/SGST/IGST split, GSTIN validation, place-of-supply, FY numbering. Tighter than the competition ships.

Product Core Wedge live

Stock → quote → order → fabrication → invoice → payment, all real Supabase data. Recovery Radar, Udhaar Radar, Slab Passport + 3D working.

Onboarding Self-serve

Setup wizard creates the tenant; Excel bulk import for customers, suppliers, products, blocks, slabs. Handover is a 2-hour job.

Security Audited

RLS tenant isolation, 4-role gates on all mutations, CSP + HSTS, rate-limited AI endpoints. All 4 audit findings fixed. 41 unit + 10 e2e tests green.

Legal Finalize

DPDP-aligned Privacy + Terms drafted and routed — but entity, jurisdiction and Grievance Officer are still placeholders. Lawyer sign-off pending.

Ops Beta-grade

Supabase backups + beta login gate work; no error monitoring (Sentry), no status page, OTP needs a production SMS provider for scale.

Payments None

No gateway. Manual UPI/bank recording only. Cannot collect subscriptions or auto-reconcile receipts.

Storefront / GTM Invisible

Root URL is a login wall. No public homepage, pricing, or demo funnel. Nobody can find or evaluate the product.

Customer Handover Journey
0

One-time prep before the FIRST paying customer

  • Fill legal placeholders + lawyer sign-off
  • Decide the price — one number
  • WhatsApp Business number as support channel
  • Add customer phones to the beta login gate (until production SMS)
  • Collect via UPI/bank against a proper GST invoice from your entity
1

Demo 30 min

  • Show the wedge, not features: Recovery Radar → Udhaar Radar → Slab Passport
  • Close: "Send me your Excel files — you're live by Friday."
2

Handover day ~2 hours

  • Get their data files BEFORE the meeting — the #1 delay
  • Setup wizard → their own isolated tenant (company, GSTIN, city)
  • Excel bulk import: customers, suppliers, products, blocks, slabs
  • Add team with roles: owner / finance / operations / fabrication supervisor
  • Acceptance test together: one real quote → order → tax invoice with THEIR GSTIN, checked against their accountant. This builds more trust than any feature tour.
3

Training 90 min, by role

  • Owner: analytics, Udhaar Radar, stock value
  • Finance: invoices, payment recording, receivables
  • Operations: stock, quotes, catalog share
  • Supervisor: mobile fabrication board on their own phone
4

Go-live + first 30 days

  • First real invoice checked by their accountant · first WhatsApp udhaar reminder sent
  • Weekly 5-min WhatsApp check-in · fix friction within 48h
  • Usage = retention: watch weekly invoice creation
  • Day 30: ask for a testimonial + 2 referrals — referrals ARE the channel in stone trade

The honesty box — say this to customers up front

No e-invoicing (IRN/QR) yet — fine below the ₹5Cr turnover threshold. No credit/debit notes, procurement (PO/GRN) or Tally export yet. Payments are recorded manually (no gateway reconciliation). Saying this unprompted builds more trust than hiding it.
