The window is OPEN. The slab-ERP problem is brilliantly solved — but every proven winner is abroad (US/EU), none is in India, none is truly mobile-first, none is in ₹. 3 · Proven ideas worth stealing from the pros

These are validated abroad — bring the best of them to India, on a phone, in ₹.

🔖 Per-slab digital identity (VavaStone)
Every slab gets a photo + dimensions + lot + location + status, tracked container-to-customer. Pairs perfectly with your phone-camera slab catalog.
📱 QR / barcode slab scan (SlabWare)
Scan a slab's code to see its details and status. Cheap to build, feels magic on a godown floor.
📦 Landed-cost-per-slab (VISCO)
Spread freight/duty/handling across a lot to show TRUE cost & profit per slab. This is the cousin of your Recovery-Rate Radar — owners love "real profit per slab."
