The one-line verdict

Nobody serves the Indian granite owner well. The global tools are world-class but cost ₹12k–₹33k/month (or ₹12.5 lakh up front) and have zero GST/India fit. The Indian tools are cheap and GST-ready but don't speak "stone." No one product is mobile-first + India-affordable + GST-smart + stone-deep — that exact box is yours.

Every rival owns part of the picture. None owns all four corners at once.

🌍 US incumbents (Moraware, Slabsmith, StoneApp)
  • ✅ Deep stone features
  • ✅ Cloud / some mobile
  • ❌ ₹12k–₹33k/mo or ₹12.5L up front
  • ❌ No GST / INR / India

  • ✅ Great GST + cheap + mobile
  • ❌ No slabs / blocks / recovery
  • ❌ No fabrication or godown
  • ❌ "Doesn't speak stone"

  • ◑ Each covers part of the box

  • Mobile-first (runs from the godown)
  • India-affordable in ₹
  • GST-smart (5% in / 18% out)
  • Speaks stone: block → slab → recovery

4 · The "wow" features that hook the owner

Each tied to a real, sourced pain — ranked. Evidence = backed by research · Hypothesis = strong bet, validate with owners.

1 Recovery-Rate Radar — "how much stone am I wasting?" Evidence
Log a block in (tonnes) → slabs out (sq-ft) → it shows recovery % per block, per machine, per supplier. The single number every granite owner obsesses over, that NO mainstream tool gives him.
😲 Shock moment: "Machine #2 wastes 8% more stone than #1 — that's ₹3.2 lakh/year." This is the moat — it's the "stone language" DONOZ pioneers and accounting tools can't touch.
2 GST Auto-Pilot + "money the govt owes you" Evidence
Buys raw at 5% (HSN 2515/2516), sells polished at 18% (HSN 6802) — the app applies the right rate automatically and tracks the input-tax-credit gap building up in his favour.
😲 Shock moment: "You have ₹1.4 lakh of GST credit sitting unclaimed." Real, current rule (effective 22 Sep 2025). Owners don't realise this themselves.
3 Live Slab Catalog from your phone Evidence
Snap each slab, tag block/lot/godown, and your customer browses a live "what's in stock" gallery — the affordable, phone-based cousin of Slabsmith's ₹12.5-lakh system.
😲 Shock moment: send a buyer a live link instead of 40 WhatsApp photos. Closes deals faster; nobody affordable does this in India.
4 Udhaar Radar + auto WhatsApp reminders Hypothesis
Who owes you, how old, auto-nudge on WhatsApp. Khatabook built a unicorn on exactly this behaviour — owners check receivables daily.
The healthy daily habit: 20-second glance at "₹ coming in." (Validate priority with real owners.)
5 Owner's Morning Card Hypothesis
One screen at 9am: cash in/out today · pending udhaar · low stock · GST due. A 30-second pulse of the whole business.
This is the "healthy addiction" — a quick daily check-in that gives control, not a doomscroll.
6 WhatsApp quote → UPI paid in 60 seconds Hypothesis
Build a quote with a slab photo, send on WhatsApp, customer taps a UPI link and pays. Meets Indian buyers exactly where they already are.
Razorpay subscription/UPI rails make this shippable. (Validate against owner workflow.)

5 · What we DON'T know yet (the honest part)

• The three India rivals (DONOZ, Reckon, MarbleTech) are evidenced only by their own marketing pages — their adoption numbers are self-reported and unverified. DONOZ is the one to watch: same niche, mobile-first, already live.

• Stone Profit System, FabSuite, ThanoSphere, SalesBinder — named in scope but no claim survived fact-checking; the big-distributor/enterprise segment of the matrix is still incomplete.

• The India pain points behind features #4–6 (udhaar, WhatsApp, vernacular Hindi/Telugu/Kannada UI) are strong bets, not yet proven by data — worth 5 phone calls to real Kishangarh/Hyderabad owners before we over-invest.
